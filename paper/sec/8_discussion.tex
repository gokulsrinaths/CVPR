\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
A conventional ablation would call the three Med-CMR systems equally image-blind; the stratified measure separates them, and the intervention shows why a headline average cannot be trusted as a statement about vision: image use depends on the strength of the nonvisual shortcut and on the strength of the visual evidence.
Where the shortcut is weak the image can change the decision and its benefit grows with its strength; where it is strong enough the decision is locked.
We recommend reporting accuracy with and without the image on the text-undecided stratum, together with its size and, for benchmark builders, the share of text-decisive questions.

\paragraph{Limits.}
\emph{Sufficiency.} The causal claim is for a planted shortcut, not for Med-CMR's own cue (three attempts to remove it failed, Supp.~B and J), and the dose-response is established on two benchmarks (PMC-VQA with three models, A-OKVQA with two); a prediction written down before the runs held for InternVL3-2B and Qwen2.5-VL-7B in two seeds, but the strength at which the decision locks differs between the benchmarks ($d=0.85$ against $0.99$ for Qwen-3B), the lock-out on A-OKVQA was observed for two seeds of Qwen-3B and for Qwen-7B only, and larger models, other shortcuts and other tasks are untested.
\emph{Not a law.} The rule for the image gap that we fitted on three conditions failed on OmniMedVQA, where a median summary misses the stratified structure.
\emph{Remedy.} Shortcut-rescue needs a text-only expert, lowers cue-present accuracy and failed on Med-CMR in five attempts (a collapse to chance, an unchanged habit and three further methods that missed a pre-registered criterion); it is a validated remedy for the controlled setting only.
\emph{Real shortcut.} Where the cue points away (235 of 3{,}000 questions) the fine-tuned models still gain nothing from the image, so the cue is not shown to be the only reason for Med-CMR's image-blindness (Supp.~X).
\emph{Method.} No patient identifiers exist (case-disjointness and near-duplicate filtering only; thresholds were set by an AI assistant's review of montages); the released adapter's training membership is unknown (pool results are labelled in-sample suspected); the analyses were implemented with an AI coding assistant under the hashed pre-registration; later runs used A30 and RTX PRO 6000 GPUs.
\emph{Small counts} bound some intervals (13--135 errors; 150--460 undecided questions).

\paragraph{Ethics and broader impact.}
We use MedReason~2026, PMC-VQA, the open-access subset of OmniMedVQA and A-OKVQA for research and redistribute no images; nothing here supports clinical use, since high benchmark accuracy does not show that a model sees the image.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
``VLMs ignore the image'' is too coarse.
A nonvisual shortcut progressively locks the decision, the visual evidence needed to unlock it grows with the shortcut, and where the shortcut is reliable enough no tested image strength helps; the strength at which this happens differs between a medical and a natural-image benchmark.
A stratified measure shows this; a headline average hides it.
